# Privacy Policy — Easy Dashcam

**Last updated:** October 2025

## Summary

Easy Dashcam collects no personal data. The app communicates only with your own dashcam over its local Wi-Fi hotspot. Nothing you view, download, or configure ever leaves your device unless you explicitly share it.

## What the app does

- Connects to your dashcam's Wi-Fi hotspot and talks to it over local TCP (no internet involved).
- Downloads recordings to your device's local storage when you ask it to.
- Reads and changes camera settings on your command.
- Stores the last-known file list and your app preferences on-device to make the app faster.

## What the app does not do

- No account, sign-up, or login of any kind.
- No analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry SDKs.
- No advertising or ad tracking.
- No internet access to any server operated by us or anyone else.
- No access to your contacts, location, photos, or other personal data — beyond the dashcam files you choose to save.

## Permissions and why they exist

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Wi-Fi / local network | Talk to the camera's hotspot. |
| Notifications | Show download progress and results. |
| Photos/videos storage | Save downloaded clips to your gallery where you can find them. |
| Foreground service + wake lock | Keep background downloads running while the screen is off. |

## Data sharing

There is none. Clips you share use Android's standard share sheet and go only to the app you pick.

## Data retention

Downloaded files stay on your device until you delete them. The app keeps a small cache (recent file list, stream temp files) in its private storage, which Android or you can clear at any time.

## Changes

Any future change to this policy will be published with the app release notes.
